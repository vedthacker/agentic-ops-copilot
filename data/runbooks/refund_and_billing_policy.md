# Refund and Billing Policy

## Approval thresholds
Support agents may approve refunds up to 500 USD. Refunds above 500 USD require manager approval. Refunds above 5,000 USD require approval from the finance controller and a documented reason.

## Duplicate charges
A duplicate charge confirmed by finance is refunded to the original payment method within 3 business days. Agents should attach the invoice number to the case.

## Timelines
Approved refunds take 5 to 7 business days to reach the customer. Enterprise contracts may define different terms, and the contract always takes priority.

## Support plans
Basic: first response to critical issues within 8 business hours. Business: within 1 hour. Enterprise: within 15 minutes with a named technical account manager.
